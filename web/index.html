About these checks

Checked

Plist structure, OpenCore 1.0.8 validation, and rules for supported desktop and Intel laptop profiles. Hardware and BIOS checks use your selections. GPU families are broad; verify your exact chip and framebuffer.

Not checked

EFI files, actual BIOS state, ACPI contents, USB wiring, and boot logs. This tool does not edit your config or guarantee a boot. Keep a backup EFI and verify changes.
